# ✦ Kiem Addons (v1.0)

A clean, modern Quality of Life (QoL) and visual enhancement mod for **Minecraft (Fabric 26.1.2 & 26.2)**.

Crafted by **gamedese**.

---

## 🌟 Features

### ✨ Animated & Static Capes
- **Smooth 24 FPS & HD Capes:** High-resolution animated and static anime capes (Sanara V1/V2/V3 and Rikka).
- **In-Game Selector:** Preview and equip capes directly inside the `F6` menu.

### 🗡 Custom Viewmodel & Swing
- **Item Positioning:** Adjust held item scale, position (X/Y/Z), and rotation.
- **Swing Speed:** Control attack animation speed without affecting your actual hits or CPS.
- **Smooth Animations:** Prevents click spam from cutting off swing animations.

### 🔍 Smooth Zoom
- **Smooth Zoom (`C`):** Cinematic zoom with mouse wheel distance control (up to 15x).
- **Cinematic Black Bars (Optional):** Movie-style letterbox bars during zoom.

### 💬 Chat QoL
- **Quick Peek (`Z`):** Hold `Z` to quickly glance at and scroll chat without stopping your movement.
- **Infinite Chat:** Removes the default 100-line chat limit.
- **Copy Message:** `Ctrl + Left Click` on any chat message to copy it to your clipboard.
- **Chat Compactor:** Automatically groups repeating messages with a `(x2, x3)` counter.

### 📜 Tooltip QoL
- **Scroll Long Tooltips:** Use mouse scroll to read long item descriptions that overflow off the screen.
- **Scale Tooltips:** `Ctrl + Scroll` to make item tooltips larger or smaller.

### 🎥 Better Third Person
- **Dynamic Camera:** Natural camera follow-lag that gives a realistic sense of speed while sprinting or flying.
- **Shoulder View (`V`):** Over-the-shoulder perspective with smooth left/right switching.

### 🎬 AFK Cinematic Camera
- **Cinematic Angles:** When you go AFK, the camera automatically switches to dynamic cinematic camera angles.
- **Instant Wakeup:** Touch your mouse or keyboard, and you're instantly back in first-person gameplay.

### 🧍 Mini Models & Anti-AFK
- **Player Scale:** Adjust your character's visual size independently.
- **Anti-AFK:** Periodic sneak, jump, or click actions to prevent idle timeouts.

### 🌐 Multi-Language
- Full support for **Türkçe 🇹🇷** and **English 🇬🇧** with a single click in the settings menu.

## ⌨️ Default Keybinds

| Key | Action |
|:---:|:---|
| **F6** | Open Settings Menu |
| **Z** (Hold) | Quick Chat Peek |
| **C** (Hold) | Smooth Zoom (Scroll to adjust) |
| **V** | Switch Shoulder View |
| **Ctrl + Scroll** | Resize Item Tooltip |

## 🛠️ Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) for Minecraft `26.2` (or `26.1.2`).
2. Download `kiems-qol-1.0-26.2.jar` from the **Releases** tab.
3. Put the jar file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch Minecraft and press **`F6`** to customize!

---

## 🏗️ Building from Source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/KMDS-a/kiem-addons.git
cd kiem-addons
./gradlew build
```
The compiled jar file will be in `build/libs/`.
